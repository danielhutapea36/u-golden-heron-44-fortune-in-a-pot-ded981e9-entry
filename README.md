## The line

> By spring you will be the tallest thing on this windowsill, and the curtain will learn to move aside.

The fortune is written for this plant in this window, not any plant in any room — the windowsill and the curtain are the plant's own world. The joke is small and lands on first read: the curtain, which has shaded the plant for months, politely learning to move aside. Nothing withers, nobody is mocked; the plant grows tall and the keeper's window rewards it.
